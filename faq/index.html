Praylendar FAQ

Questions,
answered clearly.

Everything you need to know about tracking prayers, calendar colors, privacy, friends and premium.

Is Praylendar free?

Yes. Daily prayer tracking, prayer times, reminders, month and year views, Qibla, Tasbih, friends and every export period are free. Premium adds editing of any past day, insights and trends, the year and next prayer widgets, four app themes, extra reminder sounds and a badge.

How much does Premium cost?

Premium is $4.99 a month, $29.99 a year with a 7-day free trial, or $59.99 once for lifetime access. Local prices are shown in the App Store.

Which prayers can I track?

Praylendar tracks the five daily prayers: Fajr, Dhuhr, Asr, Maghrib and Isha.

What do the calendar colors mean?

Each day deepens in shade from zero to four completed prayers, and a day with all five turns gold. Cycle days are hatched and are never counted as missed.

Does Praylendar calculate prayer times?

Yes. Praylendar calculates prayer times on your iPhone for your city, with the calculation method suggested for your country, and can remind you at prayer time or 5 to 30 minutes before. You can change the method or adjust each time by a few minutes. Your location is not sent to Praylendar's servers.

Can I log a prayer I forgot?

Yes. Log it the next day and it is marked as made up. It still counts.

What is Cycle mode?

Cycle mode lets sisters mark the days when prayer is not required. Those days are never counted as missed, your streak waits for you, and they stay on your phone.

Can friends see my prayer calendar?

No. Your full calendar remains private. Friends you add can see your profile and a simple daily prayer count, and can send you a one-tap reminder to pray. There is no chat.

Do I need an account?

No account is required for the prayer tracker. Sign in with Apple is used only when you want a profile and friends.

Can I export my calendar for free?

Yes. Week, month, 90-day, goal and annual prayer cards and stories are available to free users, in eight languages.

Which languages does Praylendar support?

English, French, Arabic, Spanish, Turkish, Indonesian, Malay and Urdu.

How do I delete my account?

Open your profile in the app and choose Delete account, or follow the steps on the account deletion page.

Is Praylendar available on Android?

Praylendar is being built for iPhone first. There is no Android release yet.
