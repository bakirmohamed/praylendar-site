Praylendar / Prayer tracker

PRAYLENDAR PRAYER TRACKER

Track the five prayers.
Keep the record simple.

A focused iPhone prayer journal designed around the exact thing you want to remember each day: which prayers you completed.

A daily checklist, not a complicated dashboard

Praylendar places Fajr, Dhuhr, Asr, Maghrib and Isha on one screen. Tap each completed prayer and save the day. The interface stays the same whether you completed one prayer or all five.

What you log

The specific prayers completed that day, plus an optional excused status when appropriate.

What you see

A color-coded monthly and yearly calendar, a gentle streak and a simple prayer count.

What stays private

Your full prayer calendar. It is only shared when you deliberately export a card.

What premium adds

The ability to return and edit older days. Core tracking and exports remain free.

Designed for honest tracking

A useful prayer record should be fast enough to maintain. Praylendar does not ask for notes, goals or a long reflection every night. You can open the app, check the prayers and leave. That lower friction is the point.

Daily reminder and gentle streak

If you want a prompt, choose one reminder time for returning to the app and completing the day. The streak marks days you come back, while a quiet gray state keeps the first day from pretending you already have a streak.

Praylendar shows prayer times calculated on your iPhone, but it is a personal tracking tool, not a religious authority. When in doubt, follow the times of your local mosque.
