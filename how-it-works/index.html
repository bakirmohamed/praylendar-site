HOW PRAYLENDAR WORKS

Five prayers.
One visual rhythm.

Praylendar keeps daily prayer tracking intentionally small: check what you completed, save the day, and let the calendar build itself.

1

Check your five daily prayers

Open today and tap Fajr, Dhuhr, Asr, Maghrib and Isha. There is no score to calculate and no long form to complete.

  • Prayer-specific rows and symbols
  • Optional excused-day status
  • One tap to save the day
2

See the month become a picture

Every tracked day receives a color based on the number of prayers completed. The monthly calendar makes consistency visible without turning worship into a dense dashboard.

See how the prayer calendar works.

3

Review and share your prayer calendar

Move from a week to a full year, then export a clean card for messages or social posts. Exports are available to every Praylendar user.

  • Week, month, 90-day and year recaps
  • Multiple export languages
  • Your calendar remains private until you share it
