Account deletion

Delete your
Praylendar account.

You can permanently remove your social profile directly from the app.

Delete from Praylendar

  1. Open Praylendar on your iPhone.
  2. Tap your avatar at the top of Today to open Me, then tap Profile under Account.
  3. Scroll to Delete account.
  4. Review the confirmation and approve the deletion.

What is deleted

Deleting your account removes your Praylendar social profile and associated cloud account data, including friend relationships and reminders, subject to limited retention required for security, fraud prevention or legal compliance.

What stays on your device

Your local prayer calendar is separate from the social profile. To remove local app data as well, use Praylendar's local reset option when available or delete the app from the iPhone after deleting your account.

Need help?

If you cannot access the app or deletion fails, contact @praylendar on X and explain that you need account-deletion support. Do not post private account details publicly.
