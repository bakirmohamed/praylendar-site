Support

How can we
help?

Answers to common questions, and the quickest way to reach the people who make Praylendar.

Message us on X

Send a direct message to @praylendar and we will get back to you. Email support is not available yet. Please do not post private account details publicly.

Open @praylendar on X

Restore a purchase

Tap your avatar at the top of Today to open Me, then tap Restore purchases. Use the Apple Account that made the original purchase.

Manage or cancel a subscription

In Me, tap Manage subscription, or open the Settings app, tap your name, then Subscriptions. Trials and refunds are handled by Apple.

Prayer times look off

Choose the calculation method your mosque follows, switch Asr to Hanafi if needed, or adjust any prayer by a few minutes. When in doubt, follow your local mosque.

Reminders are not arriving

Check that notifications for Praylendar are allowed in iOS Settings and that the prayers you want are switched on in the app. Fajr can be set to break through Focus.

Report or block someone

Tap a friend to open their page, then choose Report or Block. Reports are reviewed within 24 hours.

Delete your account

Account deletion happens inside the app. Follow the account deletion steps.
